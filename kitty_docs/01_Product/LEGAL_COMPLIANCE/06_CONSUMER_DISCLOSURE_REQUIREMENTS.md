## 1. Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) Hallmarking Disclosures

Under the Bureau of Indian Standards (Hallmarking) Regulations:
* All gold jewelry sold or redeemed through the Swastik Jewel Kitty App must carry the mandatory **BIS Hallmark**, consisting of:
  1. The official BIS logo (triangular stamp).
  2. Purity in Karat and fineness (e.g. `22K916` or `24K999`).
  3. A 6-character alphanumeric **HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification)** code laser-etched onto each item.
* Swastik Jewellers' registered BIS Hallmarking License number must be visibly disclosed in the app footer and terms modal.

## 2. Daily Benchmark Gold Rate Transparency

1. **Daily Benchmark Source**:
   - The bullion rates displayed in the sticky header ticker pill (`HeaderNavBar`) and `CoinRatesScreen` must clearly cite their pricing benchmark (e.g., *IBJA — India Bullion and Jewellers Association Daily Closing Rates*).
2. **Gold Coin Assay Charges**:
   - The breakdown between raw gold value and blister pack minting/assay charges (₹300 to ₹600) must remain transparently itemized prior to order booking.
3. **Goods & Services Tax (GST) Disclosures**:
   - All physical gold jewelry deliveries and digital gold purchases in India attract **3% GST** on gold value and **5% GST** on making charges. Digital receipts must itemize Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST) separately.
