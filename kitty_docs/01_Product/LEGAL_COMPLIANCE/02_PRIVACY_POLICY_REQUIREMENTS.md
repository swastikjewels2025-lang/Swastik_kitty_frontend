## 1. Compliance Mandate: Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023

Swastik Jewellers operates as a **Data Fiduciary** under the DPDPA 2023. The mobile application collects personally identifiable information (PII) including patron mobile numbers, names, email addresses, delivery cities, and government-issued identity documents (Aadhaar, PAN).

---

## 2. Mandatory Privacy Policy Disclosures

1. **Clear Purpose Specification**:
   - Data collected during login, profile registration, and KYC is utilized strictly for identity verification, scheme enrollment, statutory compliance (PMLA/tax), and payment processing.
2. **Itemized Data Collection Notice**:
   - Phone Number: For OTP authentication and transactional SMS alerts.
   - Full Name, Email, City: For account profile and tax invoice receipts.
   - Government ID (Aadhaar/PAN): Collected under PMLA Rule 9 statutory mandates.
3. **Data Protection / Grievance Officer**:
   - The published privacy policy must disclose the name, email, and Lucknow showroom address of Swastik Jewellers' designated Grievance Officer.
4. **Data Retention & Erasure**:
   - Financial ledger records must be retained for 5 years per Indian tax/PMLA regulations.
   - Patrons possess the right to request erasure of optional marketing data.
5. **No Third-Party Monetization**:
   - Explicit declaration that patron data is never sold, traded, or shared with third-party advertisers.
