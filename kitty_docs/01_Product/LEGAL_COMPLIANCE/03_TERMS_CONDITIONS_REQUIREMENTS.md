## 2. Mandatory Contractual Clauses

### 2.1 365-Day Delivery Guarantee
* The customer agrees to deposit 11 equal monthly installments over a period of 11 months.
* Swastik Jewellers agrees to deliver 22K or 24K gold jewelry within **365 days** from the receipt of the initial installment, complying with Rule 2(1)(c)(xii) of the Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules, 2014.

### 2.2 Characterization of Month 12 Jeweler Bonus
* The 12th installment contribution by Swastik Jewellers (e.g. ₹5,000) is legally defined as a **"Promotional Making-Charge Discount Voucher"** or **"Commercial Merchant Loyalty Rebate"** applied at the time of final jewelry invoicing.
* The bonus cannot be withdrawn as cash under any circumstances.

### 2.3 Gold Valuation & Benchmark Pricing
* At the time of maturity, physical jewelry is invoiced based on the prevailing IBJA benchmark gold rate plus applicable making charges and statutory 3% GST.

### 2.4 Forfeiture Prohibition
* Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, jewelers cannot forfeit customer principal installments due to missed payments.
* In the event of early termination, the customer retains 100% of their deposited principal, redeemable as a physical jewelry purchase voucher.
